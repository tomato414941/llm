## Interpretation

The important result is not the 15/20 score. The important result is that the
Harmony final-answer contract held across all 20 tasks:

- every response had a final marker,
- no final response was empty,
- no final response leaked `analysis` or `commentary` channel text.

The failures are mostly scoring strictness or prompt compliance issues, not
extractor failures. This is enough evidence to treat the gpt-oss eval path as
ready for a small external benchmark sample, as long as the scorer consumes
only the extracted final response.
